How Climate Accelerates GenZ Skin Aging
  • AhR Receptor Activation: Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons bound to PM2.5 bind the Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor, triggering intracellular ROS cascade and MMP-1 synthesis, degrading collagen and elastin fibers.
  • Squalene Peroxidation: Ozone and particulate matter oxidize sebum squalene into squalene monohydroperoxide (Sq-OOH), an aggressive comedogenic inflammatory agent.
  • Trans-Epidermal Water Loss (TEWL): Desiccating winter air (<35% RH) rigidifies extracellular lipid lamellae, creating micro-fissures in stratum corneum.
  • Melanocyte Dysregulation: Micro-vascular erythema triggers post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), especially prone in Fitzpatrick phototypes III–VI.
